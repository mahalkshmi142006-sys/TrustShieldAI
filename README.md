# TrustShieldAI

## AI Trust & Verification Platform

TrustShieldAI is a final-year major project focused on improving trust in AI-generated content and digital identity verification.

The project explores two major AI safety problems:

1. **LLM Hallucination Detection** – identifying unsupported or potentially false information generated by Large Language Models.
2. **Deepfake Detection** – detecting manipulated or synthetically generated facial content that may threaten digital identity verification.

## Project Objectives

- Detect and assess hallucinations in LLM-generated responses.
- Analyze the reliability of generated responses using evidence and verification techniques.
- Detect potential deepfake or manipulated facial content.
- Improve robustness against limitations of existing approaches.
- Integrate both capabilities into a unified AI trust and verification platform.
- Develop a deployable web-based application.
- Evaluate the system using appropriate datasets and performance metrics.
